The workflow

Upload once.
We handle everything after.

One pipeline from raw file to published everywhere — with AI working in the background as your video grows.

STEP 01
Upload your file
Drop an MP4, MOV, MP3 or M4A. We extract audio, run Whisper transcription, and show you the transcript live as it builds.
STEP 02
We research your niche
YouTube API pulls the top 30 videos in your space. Claude analyzes what's winning — views-per-sub, engagement ratios, hook structures.
STEP 03
Upload package generated
5 data-backed title options, full SEO description, 30 tags, auto-chapters, and an .srt captions file — all copy-pasteable and downloadable.
STEP 04
Deploy to every platform
Push your video to YouTube, then schedule TikTok clips and Instagram Reels from the same dashboard. One video, everywhere.
STEP 05
Monitor across all socials
See views, likes, comments and engagement from YouTube, TikTok and Instagram in one unified analytics panel — per video.
STEP 06
Clips detected as it grows
As comments come in and reference specific moments, we detect clip opportunities and generate a full Shorts/Reels package ready to approve.
